# Code of conduct

This project welcomes everyone who wants to learn from it, build on it, or improve it: livestream viewers, hackathon teams, and anyone else who finds it.

## Our standards

- Be respectful and patient. People here have very different levels of experience with Python, containers, Azure, and statistics.
- Discuss the code and the data, not the person. Critique is welcome; contempt is not.
- Talk about countries, teams, and peoples the way the app does: describe patterns neutrally, and make no claims about the worth of any country or people.
- No harassment, insults, discriminatory jokes or language, sexualized content, or publishing anyone's private information.

## Scope

This code of conduct applies in the repository's issues, pull requests, discussions, and any other space where someone represents the project.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the repository maintainers through their GitHub profiles, or by opening an issue that asks a maintainer to contact you, without the details. Reports are handled confidentially. Maintainers may remove comments, reject contributions, or block participants who don't follow this code of conduct.
